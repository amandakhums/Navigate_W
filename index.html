Our story

Chosen cask by cask

Founded by collector Alex de Ujfalussy and partnered with France's La Maison du Whisky, we hand-select every cask we bottle. From Waterford's terroir-driven single malts to our own exclusive NWW bottlings, every release is chosen from hundreds of samples.

What we stand for

Only the exceptionalWe bottle only the casks we're truly passionate about.
Always curiousConstantly exploring what's new in whisky, rum and fine spirits.
Whisky is about peopleThe best drams are shared, through conversation, community and great tastings.
